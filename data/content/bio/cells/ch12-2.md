# 12.2: FRQ Writing Protocols (CER)
## Claim-Evidence-Reasoning Framework for AP Biology Free Response


            <div class="lesson-section">

                <div class="key-terms-box glass" style="border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-red); padding: 20px; margin-bottom: 30px; border-radius: var(--standard-radius);">
                    <h4 style="color: var(--accent-red); margin-bottom: 12px;"><i class="fas fa-key"></i> Key Terms</h4>
                    <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 8px; font-size: 0.9rem;">
                        <div><strong>Claim</strong> — A clear, direct answer to the question asked. One sentence.</div>
                        <div><strong>Evidence</strong> — Specific data, numbers, or observations that support the claim.</div>
                        <div><strong>Reasoning</strong> — The biological principle or mechanism that connects the evidence to the claim.</div>
                        <div><strong>Justify</strong> — FRQ keyword: provide evidence AND reasoning to support a claim.</div>
                        <div><strong>Describe</strong> — FRQ keyword: provide relevant biological details about a process.</div>
                        <div><strong>Explain</strong> — FRQ keyword: describe the mechanism or cause-effect relationship.</div>
                    </div>
                </div>

                <div class="intuition-box glass" style="border-left-color: var(--accent-red);">
                    <h3><i class="fas fa-lightbulb"></i> Big Idea: Answer Like a Scientist, Not a Student</h3>
                    <p>AP Biology FRQs are not about showing everything you know. They are about <strong>precisely answering what is asked</strong>. The CER framework (Claim, Evidence, Reasoning) ensures your answer is structured, complete, and earns maximum points. Graders look for specific biological terms and logical connections — not essays.</p>
                </div>

                <h2>I. The CER Framework</h2>
                <ol style="line-height: 2.2;">
                    <li><strong>Claim:</strong> State your answer directly. Don't hedge. "The enzyme activity increased because..."</li>
                    <li><strong>Evidence:</strong> Reference specific data. "As shown in Figure 1, the reaction rate at pH 7 was 45 µmol/min compared to 12 µmol/min at pH 3."</li>
                    <li><strong>Reasoning:</strong> Connect evidence to biological principles. "This is because the enzyme has an optimal pH of 7, at which its active site maintains proper conformation for substrate binding."</li>
                </ol>

                <h2>II. FRQ Command Words Decoded</h2>
                <div style="overflow-x: auto; margin: 25px 0;">
                    <table class="glass-table" style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; font-size:0.88rem;">
                        <tr style="background: rgba(239,68,68,0.2);">
                            <th style="padding:12px; text-align:left;">Command</th>
                            <th style="padding:12px; text-align:left;">What It Means</th>
                            <th style="padding:12px; text-align:left;">What to Do</th>
                        </tr>
                        <tr><td style="padding:10px;"><strong>Describe</strong></td><td style="padding:10px;">Provide relevant details</td><td style="padding:10px;">State facts/observations with detail</td></tr>
                        <tr style="background:rgba(255,255,255,0.03);"><td style="padding:10px;"><strong>Explain</strong></td><td style="padding:10px;">Describe HOW/WHY</td><td style="padding:10px;">Give the mechanism or cause-effect</td></tr>
                        <tr><td style="padding:10px;"><strong>Justify</strong></td><td style="padding:10px;">Provide evidence + reasoning</td><td style="padding:10px;">Full CER response required</td></tr>
                        <tr style="background:rgba(255,255,255,0.03);"><td style="padding:10px;"><strong>Calculate</strong></td><td style="padding:10px;">Show math work</td><td style="padding:10px;">Formula + steps + answer with units</td></tr>
                        <tr><td style="padding:10px;"><strong>Predict</strong></td><td style="padding:10px;">What would happen if...</td><td style="padding:10px;">State outcome + reasoning based on biological principles</td></tr>
                    </table>
                </div>

                <h2>III. Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>
                <ul style="line-height: 2;">
                    <li><strong>Being vague:</strong> "The rate goes up" → Better: "The rate increased from 12 to 45 µmol/min."</li>
                    <li><strong>No reasoning:</strong> Evidence without biological explanation earns partial credit at best.</li>
                    <li><strong>Writing too much:</strong> Graders look for keywords and logic, not paragraphs.</li>
                    <li><strong>Contradicting yourself:</strong> If you write two claims, the grader uses the wrong one.</li>
                </ul>

                <div class="glass-card" style="border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-red); padding: 20px; margin-top: 30px;">
                    <h4><i class="fas fa-clipboard-check"></i> Summary</h4>
                    <ul style="line-height: 2; font-size: 0.92rem;">
                        <li>Use the CER framework: Claim (direct answer) → Evidence (specific data) → Reasoning (biological principle).</li>
                        <li>Pay attention to command words: describe, explain, justify, calculate, predict.</li>
                        <li>Be specific, use data, include biological vocabulary.</li>
                        <li>Don't contradict yourself or write unnecessary information.</li>
                    </ul>
