# 11.2: Error Analysis & SE
## Standard Deviation, Standard Error of the Mean, and 95% Confidence Intervals

            <div class="lesson-section">

                <div class="key-terms-box glass" style="border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-red); padding: 20px; margin-bottom: 30px; border-radius: var(--standard-radius);">
                    <h4 style="color: var(--accent-red); margin-bottom: 12px;"><i class="fas fa-key"></i> Key Terms</h4>
                    <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 8px; font-size: 0.9rem;">
                        <div><strong>Standard Deviation (SD)</strong> — Measures how spread out the data is from the mean.</div>
                        <div><strong>Standard Error (SEM)</strong> — Measures how far the sample mean is likely to be from the true population mean.</div>
                        <div><strong>95% CI</strong> — A range (Mean ± 2SEM) that is 95% likely to contain the true population mean.</div>
                        <div><strong>Error Bars</strong> — Graphical representation of variability; often ±2 SEM in AP Biology.</div>
                    </div>
                </div>

                <div class="intuition-box glass" style="border-left-color: var(--accent-red);">
                    <h3><i class="fas fa-lightbulb"></i> Big Idea: Mean is Not Enough</h3>
                    <p>In science, a single average number doesn't tell the whole story. We need to know the <strong>precision</strong> of that average. Standard Error tells us how much we can trust our mean. If the 95% Confidence Intervals of two groups overlap significantly, we cannot say the groups are different.</p>
                </div>

                <h2>I. The Statistics of Variation</h2>
                <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 20px; margin: 25px 0;">
                    <div class="proof-box glass">
                        <h5>Standard Deviation (s)</h5>
                        <p>$$s = \sqrt{\frac{\sum(x - \bar{x})^2}{n - 1}}$$</p>
                        <p style="font-size: 0.8rem; color: #aaa;">Shows individual variation.</p>
                    </div>
                    <div class="proof-box glass">
                        <h5>Standard Error ($SE_{\bar{x}}$)</h5>
                        <p>$$SE_{\bar{x}} = \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$$</p>
                        <p style="font-size: 0.8rem; color: #aaa;">Shows precision of the mean.</p>
                    </div>
                </div>

                <div class="glass-card" style="border-left: 4px solid var(--accent-red); padding: 20px; margin-top: 30px;">
                    <h4><i class="fas fa-clipboard-check"></i> Summary</h4>
                    <ul style="line-height: 2; font-size: 0.92rem;">
                        <li>SD measures individual data spread; SEM measures mean accuracy.</li>
                        <li>95% CI is Mean ± 2 SEM.</li>
                        <li>Non-overlapping error bars suggest a significant difference between groups.</li>
                        <li>Increasing sample size ($n$) reduces SEM, making your findings more precise.</li>
                    </ul>
